How to Drop the Projectors

  • If the projectors are still in the ceiling, use the tablet to drop them down. The option is under Panel > B1 Power, B2 Power. (The letter corresponds with which room you are in, A, B or C. These examples are for Hallowell B)
  • Tap the B1 and B2 Power buttons and the projectors should start lowering from the ceiling. Hallowell B has projector controls for rooms A and C as well. Rooms A and C only have controls for their own room. 
  • If the projectors do not lower, you may have to call IT. Sometimes, the system has to be rebooted in order to get the projectors to respond. 
  • Wait a minute or so and the projectors should begin projecting on the screen. If the projectors are lowered but not projecting anything after a minute, they may need to manually be turned on by the remote on the podium. Stand under the projector you need to turn on and press the green button on the remote.
