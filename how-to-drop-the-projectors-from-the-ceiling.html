How to Drop the Projectors from the Ceiling

  1. If the projectors are still in the ceiling, use the tablet to drop them down. The option is under Panel > B1 Power, B2 Power. (The letter corresponds with which room you are in, A, B or C. These examples are for Hallowell B
  2. Tap the B1 and B2 Power buttons and the projectors should start lowering from the ceiling. Hallowell B has projector controls for rooms A and C as well. Rooms A and C only have controls for their own room.
